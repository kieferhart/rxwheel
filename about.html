Why I built RX Wheel

After a minor back injury, I found myself managing several medications at once. Every time I tried to set a schedule I ran into the same problem: doing the mental math to space everything out evenly — while also making sure I wasn't waking up in the middle of the night to take a pill — was genuinely frustrating.

A spreadsheet didn't help. A standard pill schedule didn't help. What I needed was to see my day as a clock — where my doses fell, how evenly they were spaced, and whether any of them crept into the hours I was trying to sleep.

So I built RX Wheel. A 24-hour visual clock where you can see all your medications at once, drag doses to adjust timing, and let the optimizer automatically push everything out of your sleep window.

Who it's for

  • Anyone managing multiple medications and trying to keep them evenly spaced
  • People who want to protect their sleep and avoid middle-of-the-night doses
  • Caregivers coordinating schedules for a family member
  • Anyone who thinks visually and finds traditional pill schedules hard to reason about

How it works

  • Add each medication and set how many doses per day
  • Drag doses around the clock to find a schedule that works
  • Use the Optimize button to automatically avoid 11 PM – 5 AM
  • Export to your calendar so you never miss a dose

Privacy

RX Wheel runs entirely in your browser. No account required, no data sent to any server. Your medication schedule stays on your device.

Medical Disclaimer

Always consult your doctor, pharmacist, or other qualified healthcare provider before making any changes to your medication schedule.
